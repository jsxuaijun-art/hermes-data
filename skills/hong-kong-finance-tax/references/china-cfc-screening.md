# PRC CFC / Anti-Avoidance Screening

Screen for:
- PRC resident enterprise control;
- effective control;
- low-tax jurisdiction considerations;
- accumulation of profits;
- reasonable business needs;
- substantive business activity;
- related-party arrangements;
- transfer pricing;
- place of effective management;
- other general anti-avoidance risks.

This is a screening guide, not an automatic conclusion.
Any potential CFC or resident-enterprise issue must be verified against current PRC law and facts.
