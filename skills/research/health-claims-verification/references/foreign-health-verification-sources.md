## Public-opinion snapshot to reuse (Pew 2025 / Gallup 2024)
- 63% of Americans highly confident childhood vaccines are effective (Dems 80% vs Reps 48%).
- 53% confident in safety testing; 51% confident in schedule safety.
- Gallup 2024: 40% say vaccinating children is "extremely important" (down from 64% in 2001).
- 76% do NOT believe vaccines are more dangerous than the diseases (20% do).
- Republicans disproportionately drive the decline; "vaccine hesitancy" rose post-2020.
- Consequence on the ground: US measles cases in 2025-2026 are the highest since 1991 (~3,600+ in 2026), threatening elimination status.

## Shingrix fact pack (for doctor briefing)
- 2-dose schedule (mon 0, then 2–6 months later); 0.5 mL i.m. in deltoid.
- GBS risk: ~3–7 excess cases per million doses (post-market US studies, 65+), higher after dose 1 (~6/million), not observed after dose 2. FDA added this warning — active disclosure, not concealment.
- Not live vaccine; safe for most immunocompromised under 50 with risk; contraindicated only for severe allergy to components.
- Re-check PI revision date before quoting (e.g., "Revised: 2/2026").
