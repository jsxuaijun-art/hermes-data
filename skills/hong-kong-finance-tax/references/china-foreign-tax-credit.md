# PRC Foreign Tax Credit Checklist

When a PRC resident enterprise derives income through a Hong Kong subsidiary or other overseas entity, assess:
- income type;
- whether and when income is recognized in China;
- direct foreign tax credit;
- indirect foreign tax credit where applicable;
- ownership chain;
- underlying foreign taxes;
- credit limitation;
- carry-forward treatment if applicable;
- documentary evidence;
- treaty / arrangement relevance;
- current PRC tax-year rules.

Do not assume all Hong Kong tax saved will be immediately taxed in China.
Do not assume the opposite either.
Calculate from facts and current PRC rules.
