# China–Hong Kong Group Tax Guide

## Core principle
A Hong Kong entity-level tax saving does not automatically equal a group-level tax saving.

Always distinguish:
- Local Tax Saving
- Group Tax Saving
- Tax Deferral
- Cash-flow Benefit
- No Real Group Benefit

## Required analysis
For PRC-invested Hong Kong structures, review:
1. PRC investor identity and ownership chain
2. ODI / outbound investment compliance status
3. Equity vs shareholder loan vs other funding
4. Hong Kong entity-level tax
5. Whether profits are retained or distributed
6. PRC tax treatment of foreign-source income
7. Foreign tax credit availability
8. CFC / anti-avoidance screening
9. Place of effective management / resident-enterprise risk
10. Transfer pricing
11. Treaty / China–Hong Kong arrangement
12. Dividend / interest / royalty repatriation
13. Compliance and financing costs
14. Permanent saving vs deferral

## Group tax model
Group Tax Before Planning
- Group Tax After Planning
= Real Group Tax Benefit

Do not report “tax saving” based only on Hong Kong tax reduction.

## Output
- Hong Kong local tax effect
- PRC-side potential tax effect
- Foreign tax credit
- CFC / management / TP risk
- Repatriation effect
- Permanent saving
- Deferral benefit
- Compliance cost
- Group effective tax conclusion
