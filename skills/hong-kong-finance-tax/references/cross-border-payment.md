# 香港跨境付款分析框架

## 1. 付款类型
- Service fee
- Management fee
- Royalty
- Interest
- Cost sharing / recharge
- Dividend
- Loan principal
- Capital contribution

## 2. 必查事实
- Contract
- Invoice
- Deliverables
- Service location
- Provider / beneficiary
- Related-party status
- Payment path
- Accounting entry
- Business purpose

## 3. 双边分析
**Hong Kong Side + Counterparty Country Side + Treaty Layer + TP Layer**

香港侧不存在某类税，并不代表对方国家不存在 WHT、VAT/GST、PE、CIT 或其他义务。

## 4. 输出
- Nature of payment
- Hong Kong treatment
- Counterparty-country issues
- Treaty issues
- TP issues
- Documents
- Risk / Action
