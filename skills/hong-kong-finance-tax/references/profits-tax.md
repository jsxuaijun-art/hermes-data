# 香港 Profits Tax 分析框架

## 1. 先确认事实
- Entity and business
- Accounting period
- Revenue streams
- Expense categories
- Related parties
- Tax filing status
- Historical assessments

## 2. 分析层次
1. Accounting profit
2. Tax adjustments
3. Source of profits
4. Exempt / excluded items（如适用）
5. Related-party / TP issues
6. Losses / allowances / deductions（如适用）
7. Filing and evidence

## 3. 纪律
- 不硬编码税率或门槛；
- 先核验适用 tax year；
- Current rule 不机械套用历史年度；
- 重大判断必须保留证据链。
